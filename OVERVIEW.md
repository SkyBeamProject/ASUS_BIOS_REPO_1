# Overview

A repository of modded ASUS UEFI BIOS images for a selection of LGA 1155, LGA 1150, and LGA 2011 (Z68/Z77/B85/Z87/Z97/H97/X79) boards. Mods typically include unlocked MSR 0xE2 for the Apple power-management fix, updated CPU microcode, updated iGPU GOP, LAN PXE (Intel/Realtek/BCM), and AHCI/RAID modules (Intel/Marvell/JMicron) with RAID TRIM on 6/X79 series. Flash only the image matching your exact model.
